# Demo files

Sample files for trying the app without preparing your own data. Sign up, set up a business, then open the **My data** tab.

| File | Upload as | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| `invoices.csv` | CSV, invoices | 8 invoices saved across 5 customers |
| `expenses.csv` | CSV, expenses | 8 expenses saved from August to October 2026 |
| `invoices_broken.csv` | CSV, invoices | Nothing saved; rows 3 and 5 flagged for an invalid date and a non-numeric total |
| `pdfs/INV-*.pdf` | PDF, sales invoices I issued | Five invoices extracted for review |
| `pdfs/BILL-*.pdf` | PDF, bills from my suppliers | Five bills extracted for review |

After importing `invoices.csv` and `expenses.csv`, try these questions in **Ask your data**:

- Who owes me money?
- How much revenue did I make each month?
- What are my expenses by category?

All names, numbers and documents are fictitious.
